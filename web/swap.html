Trigger a real Bitcoin ↔ Liquid testnet HTLC atomic swap and watch it settle on-chain, step by step. Both legs complete, or the timelock refunds them.

Before you click

  • Testnet only. No real value moves. Mainnet is refused by the software itself.
  • It runs between this lab's own wallets — not yours. You are triggering a demonstration, not trading.
  • Capped. Small fixed amounts, a daily limit, and a total fee budget for the run.
  • It may pause. Testnet coins come from faucets; when a wallet runs low the demo stops until it is refilled.
  • Bitcoin confirmations set the pace. Testnet blocks are irregular — a run can take minutes or over an hour.
